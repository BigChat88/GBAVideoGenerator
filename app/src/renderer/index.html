GBA Video Generator

A GBA cartridge holds at most 32 MB.
Advanced options
Mono; ADPCM is decoded by the GBA as it plays and sounds slightly grainier.
Typically 25–55% smaller video; the GBA may drop frames above ~20 fps at the largest resolutions.
Large ROMs use the GBA Video cartridge mapper and are not compatible with all devices: they won't run on flashcarts or most emulators (mGBA plays them).
Each frame gets its own 252-color palette; dithering smooths gradients but adds noise.
