GBA Video Generator

    Add one video, or several to get a list menu on the GBA (played in this order).
    Up to 12 characters (uppercase ASCII); shown as the menu title when there are several videos.
    A GBA cartridge holds at most 32 MB.
    Advanced options
    Mono; ADPCM is decoded by the GBA as it plays and sounds slightly grainier.
    Typically 25–55% smaller video; the GBA may drop frames above ~20 fps at the largest resolutions.
    Bigger ROMs use the GBA Video cartridge mapper, which only mGBA emulates: they will not run on flashcarts or other emulators.
    Each frame gets its own 252-color palette; dithering smooths gradients but adds noise.
